Wrestling as a physical base

The physical essence
of wrestling

It is not about turning you into a wrestler. It is about taking the physical essence of wrestling and adapting it to people who want to improve their body and conditioning, whatever their starting point.

Useful strength

Functional strength applied to real movement.

Mobility

Full range of motion, without pain and with control.

Conditioning

Ability to sustain intensity for longer periods.

Coordination

Efficient, fluid and purposeful movement.

Body control

Awareness of your own body in every exercise.

The athletic experience
behind the method.

Olympic wrestling is not the end product. It is the foundation that sustains the method: discipline, body control, functional strength, endurance and real progression.

You don't need to learn wrestling or compete. Wrestling is the methodological foundation: physical principles, real progression, body control and resilience under pressure. That is what we apply to functional training.
